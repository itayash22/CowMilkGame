Drag a slate to move it; drag any edge or corner to widen or reshape it — a curtain is just a slate stretched to full width. Tears are holes in a slate that let the content show through: they move with their slate, can be dragged and resized like small windows, and are seamed closed with their ⊕ button (or a click in tear mode). Slates stick to the document, so scrolling and zooming keep them glued to the exact spot they cover.
