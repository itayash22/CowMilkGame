Keyboard shortcuts

Sspawn a slate (hides an area)
Cspawn a curtain (hides everything below)
Ltoggle spotlight (dims everything else)
Delete / ✕kill the selected slate
Xkill all slates & spotlight
Arrow keysnudge selected slate / spotlight (Shift = big steps)
+ / − , Ctrl+wheelzoom (re-rendered sharp at any level)
Wfit width  ·  0 = 100%
Ffullscreen  ·  H = hide toolbar
Oopen file  ·  Ctrl+V paste screenshot
Escdeselect / close

Drag a slate to move it. Drag any edge or corner to widen or reshape it — a curtain is just a slate stretched to full width. Slates stick to the document, so scrolling and zooming keep them glued to the exact spot they cover.
